Privacy Policy

Hansu Janggi (the "Site") has no accounts and never asks for or collects personal information such as your name or email address. It does use the outside services below for ads and visit statistics.

Effective date: December 1, 2026. This is a translation; if it differs from the Korean version, the Korean version applies.

1. Information we collect directly

The Site does not collect your personal information or store it on a server. All game calculations happen inside your browser, and your games are not sent to a server.

2. Browser storage

So that you can continue a game in progress, the moves and game settings (side, level, setups) are saved in your device's browser storage (localStorage), together with your language choice. This information stays on your device and is not sent to the Site operator. You can delete it at any time with your browser's clear site data feature.

3. Advertising

To cover running costs, the Site may use the following ad services.

  • Google AdSense (Google LLC)
  • Kakao AdFit (Kakao Corp.)

These ad services may use cookies or similar technologies to show ads based on your visits to this site and other sites. The information they collect and how they process it follow each company's privacy policy.

  • How Google uses advertising cookies: policies.google.com/technologies/ads
  • Turn off personalized Google ads: adssettings.google.com
  • Kakao privacy policy: kakao.com/policy/privacy

You can still play even if you block cookies in your browser settings.

4. Visit statistics

The Site uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits per page. It does not use cookies and only provides aggregate information that does not identify individuals.

5. Your rights

The Site holds no personal information, so there is nothing to view, correct or delete. For information processed by the ad services, you can exercise your rights as described by each company above.
